# CAD-Training Summary

A curated portfolio of 3D models, components, and assemblies developed as part of my self-directed engineering design practice. This repository showcases a mix of personal projects, practical problem-solving tools, rapid prototyping models, and exploratory drafting concepts.

---

## Technology Stack & Deliverables

* **CAD Software:** Autodesk Inventor
* **Analysis:** Structural & Stress Analysis (FEA)
* **File Formats Included:**
  * Native CAD Files (`.ipt` parts, `.iam` assemblies)
  * High-Resolution Renders & Technical Multi-Angle Views (`.png`, `.jpg`)

---

## Scope of Work

This collection represents the progression of my modeling capabilities, ranging from foundational early exercises to functional assemblies designed for real-world utility.

While these models focus primarily on functional utility and geometric experimentation, they demonstrate a solid grasp of:
* Parametric part design and sketching hierarchy
* FEA stress analysis, material assignment, and structural load simulation
* Wall thickness optimization (shelled vs. solid geometries) for additive manufacturing
* Design iteration, tolerance management, and rapid prototyping workflows

---

## Repository Structure & Project Documentation

Models in this repository are actively organized into dedicated project folders. Detailed models now include comprehensive project documentation containing:

* **Technical Specifications:** Structural variants, wall thickness, and exact millimeter/centimeter bounding dimensions.
* **Material & Simulation Data:** Load analysis parameters and material recommendations (e.g., ABS, PETG, PLA).
* **Visual Previews:** Multi-angle orthographic and isometric image grids for fast inspection without needing CAD software open.

> [!NOTE]
> **3D Printing Disclaimer:** Models are provided solely as native Autodesk Inventor CAD files (`.ipt` / `.iam`). To make these models print-ready, further processing—such as exporting to mesh formats (`.stl`, `.3mf`), adjusting slicer parameters, or verifying tolerances for your specific 3D printer—is required.

### How to Browse & Download

1. Navigate to the `Models/` directory.
2. Select a project folder (e.g., `Models/Tablet-Stand/`) to view its dedicated `README.md`, preview images, and technical breakdowns.
3. Download the native `.ipt` (or `.iam`) files directly for parametric editing, mesh exporting, or slicing preparation in your CAD/slicer software.
